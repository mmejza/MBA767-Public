Gate 2 Quiz Preview

Read this before you open the graded quiz.
Before you begin: complete the chapter reading and view the entire slide deck first. This quiz assumes that preparation — it tests whether you can apply and connect ideas from both sources, not just recall definitions.

How to Prepare

  • Complete both sources first. Students who skip one source and try to rely on the other consistently miss the synthesis questions, which require both.
  • Practice the calculations. Numeric questions require a multi-step calculation with the input values given in the question. Work through the formulas from the slides with your own numbers before you open the quiz.

Quiz Structure

  • 8 questions — 6 multiple-choice (best-answer) and 2 numeric-entry items requiring calculation (Brand Assets Score and Brand Equity).
  • Numeric questions use different input values across course sections — if you compare answers with a classmate in a different section, your correct answers may differ. This is by design.
  • 20 points total (10% of your module grade). Graded as submitted — there is no partial credit on numeric items.

Answer Format

  • Numeric items: enter the number only — no $, %, commas, or other characters. A negative sign is accepted where applicable.
  • It is your responsibility to enter answers in the exact format specified in each item. No regrade requests will be accepted for formatting errors.

Attempts

You get one attempt with a 30-minute time limit. Once you start, you must finish — there is no pause or restart, and closing the browser mid-attempt counts as your one attempt. Confirm you have 30 uninterrupted minutes before you begin.

Academic integrity: Your numeric values are individualized to your section. Do not share your calculations or answers with students in other sections.
