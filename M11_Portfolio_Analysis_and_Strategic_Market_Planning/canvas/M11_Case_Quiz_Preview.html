Case Quiz Preview

Read this before you open the graded quiz.
Before you begin: complete this module's assigned readings and learning activities first, including both apps. This quiz assumes that preparation; it does not re-teach the material.

Read the Vantage Sound Systems case narrative on the Case Overview page before you open the quiz, if you have not already. The quiz's own Instructions cover only scope and grading, not the narrative. Your individualized data appears directly inside the questions that need it. Keep App 1 and App 2 open in separate tabs.

Quiz Structure

  • 14 items testing your own assigned Vantage data (index scores and grid cells for Alpha and Beta, a three-year offensive versus defensive comparison, portfolio balance, and the grid cell for Delta) plus 1 short written recommendation plus 6 items using an independent hypothetical company (Halcyon Marine Supply); 21 items total.
  • Your data is individualized. A classmate's numbers, and therefore their correct answers, will not match yours.
  • 120 auto-graded points total: 20 scored questions at 6 points each. Question 15 is not scored automatically.

Answer Format

  • Numerical items: enter the number only; no $, %, commas, or other characters. Each item states how many decimal places to enter.
  • Index scores and net marketing contribution answers are accepted within a small window around the app's displayed value, so read the number the app shows rather than recalculating it by hand.
  • It is your responsibility to enter answers in the exact format specified in each item. No regrade requests will be accepted for formatting errors.

Attempts

You get one attempt with a 50-minute time limit. Once you start, you must finish; there is no pause or restart, and closing the browser mid-attempt counts as your one attempt. Confirm you have 50 uninterrupted minutes before you begin, and review your work before submitting; the quiz cannot be reopened once submitted.

Open-book, open-note: you may consult the textbook, slides, and your own notes while you take this quiz.
Academic integrity: Your data is individualized per student. Open materials are permitted; sharing your calculations or answers with other students is not.
