Vantage Sound Systems

Portfolio Analysis and Strategic Market Planning

Case Overview

Vantage Sound Systems is a mid-size audio equipment manufacturer with four product lines (Alpha, Beta, Gamma, and Delta) that sit in different market stages and competitive positions. Management has asked for a portfolio review using the multi-factor portfolio grid from Chapter 11 to decide where to invest, where to hold, and where to begin planning an exit.

Your task is to evaluate Vantage with the module's own methods: the Market Attractiveness Index (MAI) and Competitive Position Index (CPI) and their placement on the portfolio grid, a three-year comparison of an offensive and a defensive plan, and a portfolio-balance assessment across the four lines.

Your quiz uses individualized data. The ratings, plan inputs, and portfolio figures you need appear directly inside the questions that use them. A classmate's numbers, and therefore a classmate's correct answers, will not match yours.

How This Case Quiz Works

This case is a graded Canvas quiz, not a written submission. Questions 1 to 14 use your own Vantage data. You enter the values given in each question into the course apps, then read the result: the Alpha and Beta index scores and grid cells with App 1 (Questions 1 to 7), a three-year offensive versus defensive comparison for Beta with App 2 (Questions 8 to 12), and portfolio balance and the grid cell for Delta (Questions 13 and 14).

Question 15 asks for a short written recommendation that uses your own results from Questions 1 to 14. It is worth 0 points and is reviewed directly by your instructor; it is not scored automatically.

Questions 16 to 21 ask you to apply the same reasoning to an unrelated hypothetical company, Halcyon Marine Supply. They do not depend on your own Vantage numbers.

The quiz is worth 120 auto-graded points: 20 questions at 6 points each. Question 15 is worth 0 points.

Using the apps: open the app, click Reset, and type the values from the question into the app's own boxes.

Academic Integrity

Academic integrity note: Your data is individualized per student. Do not share your calculations or answers with other students; your numbers, and therefore your correct answers, are your own.
