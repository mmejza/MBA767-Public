Key reveal

No products in Emerging or Early Growth stages. When Pumps and Valves decline, nothing replaces them. 75% of current revenue is in markets growing ≤ 2% annually.

Takeaway

A balanced portfolio requires products at multiple life-cycle stages — not just profitable products today.
