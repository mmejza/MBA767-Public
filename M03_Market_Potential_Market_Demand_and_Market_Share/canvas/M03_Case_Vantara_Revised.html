Vantara Outdoors

Market Potential, Market Demand & Market Share

Case Overview

Vantara Outdoors is a mid-sized U.S. manufacturer of premium backcountry hiking and camping equipment: tents, sleeping bags, technical packs, and layering systems. The company sells direct-to-consumer online and through approximately 1,400 independent specialty outdoor retailers nationwide. Vantara competes exclusively at the premium end of the market, with an average selling price above the market average.

Senior leadership is debating whether Vantara should expand its internal market definition from premium backcountry camping equipment sold in the United States to a much broader outdoor-recreation category. The VP of Sales believes the current definition understates Vantara's growth opportunity. The CMO is skeptical and argues that Vantara's most urgent problem is share performance rather than market definition.

Your quiz uses individualized exhibits — your own company, market data, and share-performance figures appear directly inside each question, alongside the question that needs them. Your data is individualized: a classmate's numbers, and therefore a classmate's correct answers, will not match yours.

How This Case Quiz Works

This case is a graded Canvas quiz, not a written submission. Questions 1-10 test your own assigned company's numbers — market potential, MDI, market share, MSI, leakage, SPI, and SDI — each building on the ones before it, so complete them in order.

Questions 11-14 ask you to apply the same concepts to a separate, independent hypothetical company — not your own assigned company. These questions do not depend on your own calculations from Questions 1-10.

The quiz is worth 125 points: Questions 1-10 total 85 points, and Questions 11-14 are worth 10 points each (40 points total). Question 15 asks for a 100-150 word strategic recommendation grounded in your own Questions 1-10 numbers; it is not scored automatically but is reviewed directly by your instructor.
