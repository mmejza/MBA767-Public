Vantage Industrial

Competitive Position and Sources of Advantage

Case Overview

Vantage Industrial manufactures precision fasteners for aerospace and defense contractors. It competes in a five-firm market where positioning depends on cost structure, perceived differentiation, market share strength, and industry structure.

A competitor recently cut price by 8%, forcing Vantage to decide whether to hold or match the cut. Your analysis must integrate competitive-position calculations with industry-attractiveness and game-theory logic to produce a defensible strategic recommendation.

Your quiz uses individualized exhibits — your own assigned cost, differentiation, market-share, and Porter-force data (Exhibits 1-4) appears directly inside the questions that need it. Your data is individualized: a classmate's numbers, and therefore a classmate's correct answers, will not match yours.

How This Case Quiz Works

This case is a graded Canvas quiz, not a written submission. Questions 1-7 test your own assigned Vantage Industrial numbers — the Variable Cost Advantage Index (VCAI), three differentiation benefit scores and their sum (the Differentiation Index), Relative Market Share (RMS), and the Industry Attractiveness Index (IAI).

Questions 8-11 ask you to apply the same reasoning to an unrelated hypothetical company, Solenoid Precision — not Vantage Industrial. These questions do not depend on your own Questions 1-7 numbers. Question 12 is a short reflection on your own Vantage Industrial numbers' strategic implication; it is not scored automatically.

The quiz is worth 125 points: Questions 1-7 total 85 points, and Questions 8-11 are worth 10 points each (40 points total). Question 12 is worth 0 points and is reviewed directly by your instructor, not auto-graded.

Academic Integrity

Academic integrity note: Your exhibit data is individualized per student. Do not share your calculations or answers with other students — your numbers, and therefore your correct answers, are your own.
