Case Quiz Preview

Read this before you open the graded quiz.
Before you begin: complete this module's assigned readings and learning activities first. This quiz assumes that preparation — it does not re-teach the material.

The Vantage Industrial case narrative and background appear inside the quiz itself, at the top of its Instructions. You do not need to visit a separate case page first — this Preview page is the only thing to read before you open the quiz.

Quiz Structure

  • 7 numerical-entry items (VCAI, three differentiation benefit scores, Differentiation Index, RMS, and IAI, testing your own assigned exhibit data) + 4 multiple-choice items (interpretation, using an independent hypothetical company) + 1 short reflection item — 12 items total.
  • Your Exhibit 1-4 data is individualized. A classmate's numbers — and therefore their correct answers — will not match yours.
  • 125 points total: Questions 1-7 total 85 points; Questions 8-11 are worth 10 points each (40 points). Question 12 is not scored automatically.

Answer Format

  • Numerical items: enter the number only — no $, %, commas, or other characters.
  • It is your responsibility to enter answers in the exact format specified in each item. No regrade requests will be accepted for formatting errors.

Attempts

You get one attempt. Once you start, you must finish — there is no pause or restart, and closing the browser mid-attempt counts as your one attempt. Review your work before submitting — the quiz cannot be reopened once submitted.

Time limit: to be confirmed by your instructor before this quiz is deployed. Comparable modules' Case quizzes of this length use a 45-minute limit.
Open-book, open-note: you may consult the textbook, slides, and your own notes while you take this quiz.
Academic integrity: Your data is individualized per student. Open materials are permitted; sharing your calculations or answers with other students is not.
