Same Two-Point Rule as Module 4

Ch. 4 states it as "more than one point" for whole-number ratings, where that equals at least 2. With decimal ratings the rule stays at least 2.0: a 1.3 or 1.9 gap earns nothing.

Threshold Trap

Outperforming a competitor by less than 2.0 points earns ZERO advantage. This is a course convention: gaps under 2.0 are treated as parity, because small rating differences may not reflect a real customer-perceived gap. A score of +1.9 and a score of −1.9 both contribute zero to the index.
