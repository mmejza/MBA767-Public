Reading the Three Indices Together
Combining Product, Service, and Brand scores shows where the firm leads, where it is at parity, and where it trails
Product Diff. Index
> 100
Firm has genuine performance advantage on key customer benefits — invest to defend and widen this gap
Service Quality Index
= 100
Parity — no service advantage to defend or exploit. Service is not hurting the firm, but it is not helping either.
Brand Advantage Index
= 100
Parity — brand is not a differentiator at current ratings. Both brand dimensions are within the threshold.
All Three Together
Shows where the firm leads (above 100), where it is at parity (100) and where it trails (below 100), so investment goes where a gap can be opened or closed
Strategic Reveal

Three index scores together tell a firm where its competitive advantage actually lives (above 100), which dimensions are at parity (100) and could become differentiators if ratings improve by at least 2.0, and where it trails (below 100).
