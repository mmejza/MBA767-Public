Apex Industrial

The Customer Experience and Value Creation

Case Overview

Apex Industrial manufactures commercial industrial equipment. Its flagship product competes in a market where customers evaluate systems on a combination of lifecycle cost, performance, and total cost of ownership. The data in this quiz come from a three-year market analysis and a customer preference survey conducted with contractors.

Apex is deciding whether it should increase price, defend current pricing, or adjust its product strategy based on the economic value it creates, the customer value perception it projects, and the conjoint-based tradeoffs revealed by the contractor survey.

Your quiz uses individualized exhibits — your own assigned company, product, and exhibit data appear directly inside each question, alongside the question that needs them. Your data is individualized: a classmate's numbers, and therefore a classmate's correct answers, will not match yours. Every version contains one intentionally tricky sign logic issue in the cost-of-purchase analysis — read carefully before calculating.

How This Case Quiz Works

This case is a graded Canvas quiz, not a written submission. Questions 1-9 test your own assigned company's numbers — Economic Value, Indifference Price, Headroom, PPI, OPI, CPI, PCV Index, and both Conjoint CVI results — each building on the ones before it, so complete them in order.

Questions 10-13 ask you to apply the same concepts to four separate, independent hypothetical companies — not your own assigned company. These questions do not depend on your own calculations from Questions 1-9.

The quiz is worth 125 points: Questions 1-9 total 85 points, and Questions 10-13 are worth 10 points each (40 points total). Question 14 asks for a 100-150 word strategic recommendation grounded in your own Questions 1-9 numbers; it is not scored automatically but is reviewed directly by your instructor.
