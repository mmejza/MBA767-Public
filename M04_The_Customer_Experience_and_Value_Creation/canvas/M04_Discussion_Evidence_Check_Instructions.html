What This Quiz Is

One multiple-choice question, worth 5 points — the remaining 15 points of the Discussion are the post itself, graded separately. This question uses a scenario independent of Apex Industrial, so it does not require you to have finished the Discussion post first.

It checks whether you can apply this module's PCV Index sign logic (superior value zone vs. inferior value zone) to new data, not whether you remember a specific fact from the reading.
