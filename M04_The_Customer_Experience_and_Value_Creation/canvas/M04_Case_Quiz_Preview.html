Case Quiz Preview

Read this before you open the graded quiz.
Before you begin: complete this module's assigned readings and learning activities first. This quiz assumes that preparation — it does not re-teach the material.

The Apex Industrial case narrative and background appear inside the quiz itself, at the top of its Instructions. You do not need to visit a separate case page first — this Preview page is the only thing to read before you open the quiz.

Quiz Structure

  • 9 numerical-entry items (calculation chain, testing your own assigned company's numbers) + 4 multiple-choice items (interpretation, each using a separate independent hypothetical company) + 1 short strategic-recommendation item — 14 items total.
  • Your exhibit data is individualized. A classmate's numbers — and therefore their correct answers — will not match yours.
  • 125 points total: Questions 1-9 total 85 points; Questions 10-13 are worth 10 points each (40 points total). The written recommendation is not scored automatically.

Answer Format

  • Numerical items: enter the number only — no $, %, commas, or other characters. A negative sign is accepted where applicable.
  • It is your responsibility to enter answers in the exact format specified in each item. No regrade requests will be accepted for formatting errors.

Attempts

You get one attempt. Review your work before submitting — the quiz cannot be reopened once submitted.

Academic integrity: Your data is individualized per student. Do not share your calculations or answers with other students.
