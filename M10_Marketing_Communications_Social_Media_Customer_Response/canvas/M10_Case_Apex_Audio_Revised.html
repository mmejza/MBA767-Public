Apex Audio

Marketing Communications, Social Media, and Customer Response

Case Overview

Apex Audio is launching the Apex ONE wireless speaker at a $149 MSRP in a competitive mid-price segment. The brand operates through both direct consumer channels (pull) and regional distributors (push). A pilot campaign generated stage-level customer response data, and management is now evaluating national media rollout economics, annual advertising budget effectiveness under carryover, and a proposed one-month distributor promotion.

Your task is to evaluate this situation with the module's own methods: the Customer Response Index (CRI) hierarchy and stage-leverage diagnosis, Gross Rating Points (GRP) and media cost math, advertising carryover and short-run vs. total-impact ROI, and a 3-month trade-promotion gross-profit evaluation.

Your quiz uses individualized data. Your own assigned CRI stage rates, media plan parameters, carryover inputs, and trade-promotion figures appear directly inside the questions that need them. A classmate's numbers, and therefore a classmate's correct answers, will not match yours.

How This Case Quiz Works

This case is a graded Canvas quiz, not a written submission. Questions 1-15 test your own assigned Apex Audio numbers across all four parts of the case: compound CRI and stage-leverage diagnosis (Part 1), national media plan GRP and cost, and whether it addresses the diagnosed CRI weakness (Part 2), advertising carryover current-period and total-impact sales, profit, and ROI (Part 3), and 3-month trade-promotion gross profit versus a no-promotion baseline (Part 4).

Questions CQ1-CQ5 ask you to apply the same reasoning to an unrelated hypothetical company, Vireo Kitchen Co., not Apex Audio. These questions do not depend on your own Q1-Q15 numbers. Question 16 asks for a short written recommendation using your own Apex Audio numbers; it is not scored automatically.

The quiz is worth 100 auto-graded points: Questions 1-15 total 75 points, and Questions CQ1-CQ5 total 25 points. Question 16 is worth 0 points and is reviewed directly by your instructor, not auto-graded.

Academic Integrity

Academic integrity note: Your data is individualized per student. Do not share your calculations or answers with other students; your numbers, and therefore your correct answers, are your own.
