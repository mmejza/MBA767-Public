Reflect on Your Results - No AI

Submit your reflection in the separate Reflect on Your Results assignment before using AI. Keep it unchanged. The limit is 150 words; its 10 points are scored within the 40-point planning and adaptation assignment.

Resource Allocation Plan - Initial Submission - AI Permitted

Submit your initial allocation explanation (up to 200 words) and the Initial Plan column of the Financial Table. This submission is required and assessed within the single 40-point planning assessment. Submit this initial plan and then proceed to Part 3: Changed Budget.

Resource Allocation Plan - Updated Plan and Decision Rationales - AI Permitted

Submit the following together in the Resource Allocation Plan - Updated Plan and Decision Rationales assignment:

  • Decision Rationale 1 and Decision Rationale 2: up to 125 words each, including brief AI excerpts. The two rationales cover both planning stages together.

  • Financial Table: use the eight-row template on the assignment, with Initial Plan and Updated Plan figures side by side. Full prompts are provided there.

The combined written ceiling is 750 words, including your separately saved reflection, both rationales, initial and updated plan explanations, plus the compact financial table. These are maximums, not targets. You do not need to copy your earlier submissions, supply Canvas record links, or submit a full AI transcript or hours log.
