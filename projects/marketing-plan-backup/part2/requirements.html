MBA 767 · Marketing Plan Project

Part 1: Overview & Requirements

Purpose

Recommend which customers Alder Home should target, the positioning and product benefits it should emphasize, and how it should price, sell and promote its products. Alder Home is a fictional home-comfort business with products and an optional support membership. Each quiz question supplies the facts it needs.

How the Modules Fit Together

This project has two parts and one shared final quiz. Part 1 develops a marketing strategy; Part 2 develops and updates a resource allocation plan. The shared final New Scenario Quiz appears only at the end of Part 2, after updated-plan submission. Follow this module’s items in order; no stage requires waiting for instructor grading.

Writing and Submission Requirements

Reflect on Your Results - No AI

Submit your reflection in the separate Reflect on Your Results assignment before using AI. Keep it unchanged. The limit is 150 words; its 15 points are scored within the 60-point Marketing Recommendation.

Marketing Recommendation - AI Permitted

Submit the following together in the Marketing Recommendation assignment:

  • Decision Rationale 1 and Decision Rationale 2: up to 125 words each, including brief AI excerpts.
  • Recommendation: up to 200 words.
  • Financial Table: use the three-row template on the assignment. Full prompts are provided there.

The combined written ceiling is 600 words, including your separately saved reflection and both rationales, plus the compact financial table. These are maximums, not targets. You do not need to copy your earlier submissions, supply Canvas record links, or submit a full AI transcript or hours log.
