Part 1: Project Overview and Requirements

Purpose

Analyze Alder Home’s customer, financial and market information, then recommend which customer or market issue deserves priority. Alder Home is a fictional home-comfort business with products and an optional support membership. Each quiz question supplies the facts it needs.

How the modules fit together

Each part has two modules. Complete the analysis and recommendation first. Then complete the New Scenario Quiz during its scheduled availability window. Both modules count toward that part’s 100 points.

No AI and AI Permitted

  • Self-Assessment — Without Assistance or References: no AI, another person’s help, textbook, notes, worked examples or other references. This asks about your expectations, not for calculations.
  • Analysis Quiz and New Scenario Quiz — No AI: assigned references, your notes, your own templates/worked examples, a calculator and ordinary spreadsheets are permitted. Disable AI features. Do not use AI, another person’s help or work, or shared project answer sheets.
  • Review Your Results — No AI: write and submit your own reflection before using AI. Keep the saved reflection unchanged.
  • Marketing Recommendation — AI Permitted: after submitting the required reflection, you may use AI and discuss the case with classmates. Submit your own work and check consequential claims through your own calculations or evidence. Asking the same AI or another AI for agreement does not count as verification.

Writing and submission requirements

  • Reflection: up to 150 words, saved separately before AI.
  • Decision Rationale 1 and Decision Rationale 2: up to 125 words each, including brief AI excerpts.
  • Marketing Recommendation: up to 200 words.
  • Combined written ceiling: 600 words, including the saved reflection and both rationales, plus the compact financial table. These are maximums, not targets.
  • No full AI transcript, hours log, copied earlier submission or student-supplied Canvas record links are required. Full prompts and the five-row table template are on the Marketing Recommendation assignment.
