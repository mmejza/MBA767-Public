Part 1: Using AI to Develop Your Recommendation

When AI is permitted

You may use AI after submitting your Reflect on Your Results assignment.

During the AI-permitted stage, you may use an AI assistant of your choice and discuss the case with classmates. No paid AI subscription is required (UNLV provides students with access to a Claude account; see Getting Started with Claude at UNLV for sign-in instructions). Write your own submission.

Give AI Your Completed Work

Give the AI assistant the case, assignment instructions, your unchanged Analysis Quiz answers, prepared feedback, self-assessment, and submitted reflection. You can begin with:

I’m preparing a marketing recommendation for Alder Home. Here are the case, my original answers, the feedback and my reflection. Help me examine my reasoning. Separate facts from assumptions, question claims that lack support, and do not invent missing information. Wait for my first question.

Check AI Claims Yourself

AI can help you question your reasoning, identify weaknesses, and explore alternatives. However, its responses may contain errors, unsupported claims, or questionable assumptions.

Before using an AI-generated claim to support your recommendation, check it independently:

  • Numerical claims: Recalculate using the case data and permitted non-AI tools.
  • Factual claims: Compare them with the case or assigned materials.
  • Assumptions and interpretations: Identify what is uncertain and consider whether a different assumption would change your recommendation.

Asking the same or another AI assistant to confirm a claim does not count as independent verification.

In each of your two decision rationales, briefly explain what you checked, how you checked it, what you found, and how it affected your decision. Include only the AI exchanges relevant to your two required rationales. A complete conversation transcript is not required.
