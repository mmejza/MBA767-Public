Part 1: Using AI in Your Analysis

When AI is permitted

During the AI-permitted stage, you may use an AI assistant of your choice and discuss the case with classmates. No paid AI subscription is required (UNLV provides students with access to a Claude account; see Getting Started with Claude at UNLV for sign-in instructions). Write your own submission.

Start with your work

Once you reach this stage, give the assistant the case, assignment instructions, your unchanged original answers, prepared feedback, self-assessment, and saved reflection. You can begin with:

I’m preparing a marketing recommendation for Alder Home. Here are the case, my original answers, the feedback and my reflection. Help me examine my reasoning. Separate facts from assumptions, question claims that lack support, and do not invent missing information. Wait for my first question.

Check claims yourself

Ask about something you want to understand or test. For numerical claims, perform the relevant calculation yourself using permitted non-AI tools. For other claims, check the case facts or assigned references, or test the claim against an explicit assumption or alternative. Explain what you checked and what you found.

Asking the same AI or another AI to agree does not count as verification. You may agree with the assistant; what matters is whether you can support the decision. Include only the brief exchanges needed for your two rationales. A full conversation transcript is not required.
