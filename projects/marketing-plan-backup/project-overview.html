Marketing Plan Project

For this project, you will advise Alder Home, a fictional business, using what you learned in Modules 1-11 to assess its situation, recommend a marketing strategy, and decide how it should spend its budget.

The Three Parts

  • Part 1 - Situation Analysis: use customer, market, and financial information to identify what Alder Home should address first.
  • Part 2 - Marketing Strategy: recommend which customers Alder Home should target, the positioning and product benefits it should emphasize, and how it should price, sell, and promote its products.
  • Part 3 - Resource Allocation and Contingency Planning: choose how to spend the available budget, explain your choices, and revise your plan when business conditions change.

How the Modules Fit Together

Each part ends with a New Scenario Quiz, where you independently apply what you learned to changed facts after completing your recommendation or planning work.

The first two New Scenario Quizzes appear with the next part’s work to keep the sequence together and reduce the number of Canvas modules:

  • Part 1’s preparation page and New Scenario Quiz are at the beginning of the Part 2 module.
  • Part 2’s preparation page and New Scenario Quiz are at the beginning of the Part 3 module.
  • Part 3’s New Scenario Quiz appears at the end of Part 3, after you submit your revised plan.

Complete the opening preparation and quiz in the Part 2 and Part 3 modules before starting the next part’s main work.

Each quiz is worth 25 points toward its original part. For example, the Part 1 New Scenario Quiz counts toward Part 1’s 100 points, even though you take it in the Part 2 module.

No AI and AI Permitted

The activity labels tell you when AI is allowed. Permission applies only to the activities labeled AI Permitted; it does not carry over to later quizzes.

No AI: Complete the self-assessment, Analysis Quiz, Reflect on Your Results reflection, and New Scenario Quiz independently, without AI or another person’s help. Follow each activity’s instructions about permitted references and tools. No AI does not automatically mean closed-book.

AI Permitted: After submitting the required independent work and reflection, you may use AI and discuss the case with classmates for the recommendation, practice, and planning activities carrying this label. Keep your original independent submissions unchanged. Write your own submission and be ready to explain your choices.

Check any AI claim that affects your recommendation. Do the calculation yourself, check the case facts or assigned references, or explain how you tested the reasoning. Asking AI whether its answer is right does not count as a check.
