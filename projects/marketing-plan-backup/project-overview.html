MBA 767

Marketing Plan Project

Advise Alder Home, a fictional home-comfort business, using what you learned in Modules 1-11 to recommend a marketing strategy and decide how to allocate its budget.

The Two Parts

  • Part 1 - Marketing Strategy: use the supplied case background and strategy evidence to recommend customers, positioning, product benefits, price, channels and promotion.
  • Part 2 - Resource Allocation and Contingency Planning: choose how to spend the available funding, explain the trade-offs, and update your plan when the budget changes.

How the Modules Fit Together

Follow each module’s items in order. Each part begins with a self-assessment and an independent initial quiz, followed by prepared feedback, your reflection, and AI-permitted development. You do not need to wait for instructor grading.

There is one shared final New Scenario Quiz, at the end of Part 2 after your updated plan and decision rationales. It assesses both strategy and allocation using new facts. There is no separate scenario quiz at the end of Part 1 or beginning of Part 2.

No AI and AI Permitted

Complete Before You Begin without assistance or references. Complete each initial quiz and reflection independently, without AI or another person’s help. Quiz instructions specify permitted references, calculators and ordinary spreadsheets; disable AI features.

After submitting the required reflection, you may use AI and discuss the case with classmates for activities labeled AI Permitted. Write your own work and independently check claims that affect your decisions. Asking AI for agreement does not count as verification.

The final New Scenario Quiz is independent. You may consult assigned references and your own notes, templates and worked examples prepared before the quiz, including work from the AI-permitted stage. Close AI tools. Do not ask AI or another person to calculate, interpret, check or answer quiz questions. Another person’s work and shared project answer sheets are prohibited.
