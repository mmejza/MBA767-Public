Marketing Plan Project

For this project, you will advise Alder Home, a fictional business, using what you learned in Modules 1–11 to assess its situation, recommend a marketing strategy, and decide how it should spend its budget.

The three parts

  1. Situation Analysis: use customer, market, and financial information to identify what Alder Home should address first.
  2. Marketing Strategy: recommend which customers Alder Home should target, the positioning and product benefits it should emphasize, and how it should price, sell, and promote its products.
  3. Resource Allocation and Contingency Planning: choose how to spend the available budget, explain your choices, and revise your plan when business conditions change.

Working through Canvas

Each part has two modules listed together in Canvas. The first contains the analysis and recommendation or planning work. The second contains the New Scenario Quiz. Complete them in that order, and check the dates for both. Together, they are worth 100 points.

Start with the self-assessment and Analysis Quiz. Then read the prepared feedback and submit your reflection before using AI for the recommendation or planning assignment. Keep your original answers and reflection unchanged. Finish with the New Scenario Quiz.

No AI and AI Permitted

No AI: Complete the Analysis Quiz, Review Your Results reflection, and New Scenario Quiz on your own, without AI or another person’s help. The self-assessment is also completed without assistance; follow its instructions about references. No AI does not automatically mean closed-book. Each activity tells you which references, notes, calculators, or other non-AI tools you may use.

AI Permitted: You may use AI and discuss the case with classmates for the recommendation and planning activities carrying this label. First, submit the required independent work and reflection. Keep those originals unchanged. Write your own submission and be ready to explain your choices.

In an AI Permitted activity, check any AI claim that affects your recommendation. Do the calculation yourself, check the case facts or assigned references, or explain how you tested the reasoning. Asking AI whether its answer is right does not count as a check.

The New Scenario Quiz — No AI follows the recommendation or planning work. Complete each part’s New Scenario Quiz before starting an AI Permitted activity in the next part. If you missed initial work, follow the separate continuation instructions in Canvas.

Points and schedule

The project is worth 300 points—100 points per part. In each part, the self-assessment is worth 5 points, the Analysis Quiz 30, the recommendation or planning assignment 40, and the New Scenario Quiz 25. Your reflection is submitted separately, but its 10 points are included in the 40-point assignment.

Getting started

In the Part 1 Canvas module, read Project Overview & Requirements, then complete the self-assessment. You will find the questions, feedback, and submission instructions in Canvas. Submit all work there.

If you miss the initial work, you can still use the preparation materials and take the New Scenario Quiz when that module opens. You can earn points for the new quiz; it does not replace the missed work or restore its points. If you have a valid reason for missing a deadline, contact the instructor to request an exception.
