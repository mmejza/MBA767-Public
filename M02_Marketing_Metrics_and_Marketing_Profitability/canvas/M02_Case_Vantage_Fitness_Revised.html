Vantage Fitness

Marketing Metrics & Marketing Profitability

Case Overview

Vantage Fitness is a regional fitness and wellness company operating 18 studio locations across the Pacific Northwest. The company offers three service lines: group fitness classes, personal training, and studio equipment rental. Vantage competes in a regional boutique fitness market that has grown at 18% annually for the past three years.

Despite reporting revenue growth each year, Vantage's leadership team is concerned about strategic performance. The company has engaged your consulting team to conduct a full marketing metrics analysis and provide a specific, quantitatively grounded strategic recommendation.

Your task is to analyze the exhibits in your assigned quiz version and reach conclusions grounded in specific calculations, not generic marketing advice.

How This Case Quiz Works

This case is a graded Canvas quiz, not a written submission. Your quiz uses individualized exhibits (customer satisfaction distribution, income statement, product-line data, complaint-resolution program parameters, and CLV inputs) — the specific numbers you need appear directly inside each question, alongside the question that needs them. Your data is individualized — a classmate's numbers, and therefore a classmate's correct answers, will not match yours.

Questions 11-14 ask you to apply the same concepts to a separate hypothetical company, Harborview Wellness Group — not Vantage Fitness. These questions do not depend on your own Vantage Fitness calculations.

The quiz is worth 120 points: Questions 1-10 range from 5-10 points each (70 points total), and Questions 11-14 are worth 12.5 points each (50 points total). Question 15 asks you to briefly explain your reasoning for Question 14; it is not scored automatically but may be reviewed by the instructor.

Academic Integrity

Academic integrity note: Your exhibit data is individualized per student. Do not share your calculations or answers with other students — your numbers, and therefore your correct answers, are your own. Not everyone reaches the same answers.
