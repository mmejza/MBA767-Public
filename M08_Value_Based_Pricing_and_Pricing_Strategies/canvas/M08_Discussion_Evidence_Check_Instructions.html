Discussion Evidence Check Quiz

Auto-graded companion to the Discussion post · 5 points

What This Quiz Is

One multiple-choice question, worth 5 points: the remaining 15 points of the Discussion are the post itself (“The VP of Sales vs. the CFO”), graded on completion. This question uses a scenario independent of the Discussion post's own company, so it does not require you to have finished the Discussion post first.

It checks whether you can compare a market's elasticity-implied volume change to the break-even volume requirement and reach the right conclusion, not whether you remember a specific fact from the reading.

What to Expect

  • One attempt. Answer choices are shuffled, so option order will not match a classmate's.
  • After submission, you see your score. The correct answer is not displayed.
  • No calculation is required: the elasticity-implied volume change and the break-even requirement are both given directly. The question tests how you interpret and compare them, not your arithmetic.
