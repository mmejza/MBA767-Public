Vantara Medical Devices

Value-Based Pricing and Pricing Strategy

Case Overview

Vantara Medical Devices manufactures the VX-900 surgical imaging system for mid-sized hospitals. The VX-900 carries a price premium over a leading competitor, and several hospital deals have stalled on price. The VP of Sales recommends a price cut; the CFO disagrees.

Your task is to evaluate this decision with the module's own methods: economic value analysis, performance-value (conjoint) scoring, and break-even pricing analysis, and to determine whether the premium is justified, whether a cut is financially sound, and what pricing strategy should guide Vantara's next moves.

Your quiz uses individualized data. Your own assigned ownership-cost, conjoint, and pricing/market figures appear directly inside the questions that need them. Your data is individualized: a classmate's numbers, and therefore a classmate's correct answers, will not match yours.

How This Case Quiz Works

This case is a graded Canvas quiz, not a written submission. Questions 1-10 test your own assigned Vantara numbers: the competitor's and the VX-900's own 5-year ownership cost, the VX-900's weighted value score and value advantage, and the break-even percentage, elasticity-implied volume change, break-even volume, and break-even market share for the VP's proposed price cut.

Questions 11-14 ask you to apply the same reasoning to an unrelated hypothetical company, Solara Diagnostics, not Vantara. These questions do not depend on your own Questions 1-10 numbers. Question 15 is a short reflection on your own Vantara numbers' strategic implication; it is not scored automatically.

The quiz is worth 100 points: Questions 1-10 total 75 points, and Questions 11-14 total 25 points. Question 15 is worth 0 points and is reviewed directly by your instructor, not auto-graded.

Academic Integrity

Academic integrity note: Your data is individualized per student. Do not share your calculations or answers with other students; your numbers, and therefore your correct answers, are your own.
