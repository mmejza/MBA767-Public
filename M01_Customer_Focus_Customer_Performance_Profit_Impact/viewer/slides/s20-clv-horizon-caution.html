These Do Not Converge — And That's Expected

At 5 periods you've captured $165.74 of the $408.89 total infinite-horizon PV of profits — just 40%. The closed-form estimate assumes the relationship continues indefinitely; the period-by-period estimate only reflects however many periods you choose to project. The two methods converge only once the untruncated "tail" becomes economically negligible — and with retention this high relative to the discount rate (r ÷ (1+i) = 0.911, vs. 0.727 on Slide 19), that tail stays large for many more periods than 5. Always state your horizon when reporting a period-by-period CLV estimate.
