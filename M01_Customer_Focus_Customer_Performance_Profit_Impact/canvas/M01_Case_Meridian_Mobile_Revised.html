Meridian Mobile

Customer Focus, Customer Satisfaction, Retention Economics, and Customer Lifetime Value

Case Overview

Meridian Mobile is a regional mobile virtual network operator (MVNO) operating in a competitive urban market. The company recently completed a customer satisfaction survey and is evaluating how customer satisfaction, retention behavior, and profitability should shape its customer strategy.

Meridian’s leadership team is considering whether to invest in a complaint-resolution program, how to interpret profitability differences across customer segments, and what customer lifetime value benchmark should guide future acquisition spending.

Your task is to analyze the exhibits in your assigned quiz version and reach conclusions grounded in specific calculations, not generic marketing advice.

How This Case Quiz Works

Your quiz uses individualized exhibits (satisfaction-tier distribution, subscriber count, retention economics, and complaint-resolution program parameters) — the specific numbers you need appear directly inside each question, alongside the question that needs them. Your data is individualized — a classmate's numbers, and therefore a classmate's correct answers, will not match yours.

Quizzes are worth 125 points: Q1-8 range from 5-10 points each (65 points total), and Q9-12 are worth 15 points each (60 points total).

Academic Integrity

Academic integrity note: This case uses original fictional data, individualized per student. Do not share your calculations or your answer choices with other students — your numbers, and therefore your correct answers, are your own. Not everyone reaches the same answers.
