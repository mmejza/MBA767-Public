Case Quiz Preview

Read this before you open the graded quiz.
Before you begin: complete this module's assigned readings and learning activities first. This quiz assumes that preparation — it does not re-teach the material.

The Meridian Mobile case narrative and background appear inside the quiz itself, at the top of its Instructions. You do not need to visit a separate case page first — this Preview page is the only thing to read before you open the quiz.

Quiz Structure

  • 8 numerical-entry items (calculation gate) + 4 multiple-choice items (interpretation) + 1 short written-reflection item — 13 items total.
  • Your exhibit data is individualized. A classmate's numbers — and therefore their correct answers — will not match yours.
  • 125 points total: Q1-8 range from 5-10 points each (65 points total); Q9-12 are worth 15 points each (60 points total). The reflection item is not scored automatically.

Answer Format

  • Numerical items: enter the number only — no $, %, commas, or other characters. A negative sign is accepted where applicable.
  • It is your responsibility to enter answers in the exact format specified in each item. No regrade requests will be accepted for formatting errors.

Attempts

You get one attempt with a 45-minute time limit. Once you start, you must finish — there is no pause or restart, and closing the browser mid-attempt counts as your one attempt. Confirm you have 45 uninterrupted minutes before you begin, and review your work before submitting — the quiz cannot be reopened once submitted.

Open-book, open-note: you may consult the textbook, slides, and your own notes while you take this quiz. Several numeric items build on your answer to an earlier item in the same quiz — if you catch a mistake, you will need time to correct it and rework anything downstream, so budget your time with that in mind.
Academic integrity: Your data is individualized per student. Open materials are permitted; sharing your calculations or answers with other students is not.
