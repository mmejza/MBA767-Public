The Post
Part 1 · Introduce Yourself

Your name, your background, and one product or brand you personally have strong opinions about.

Part 2 · Need → Want

Using this week's Need → Want → Demand → Market vocabulary, break down one purchase you made in the past year. What was the underlying need? Why did you choose that particular want over the alternatives available to you?

Part 3 · Beyond the Quiz

Pick one takeaway from the practice quiz and argue for it — either:

(a) a need you thought of for one of the three products that wasn't among the answer options, or

(b) a segment-attractiveness claim where you still disagree with the feedback, and why.

Why Part 3 exists: the quiz format constrained you to a fixed list of options. This is where you get to go past it — with your own reasoning, not a checkbox.
Replies (Two Classmates)

Does their need/want breakdown match how you'd have framed it? And does their Part 3 takeaway change how you'd have answered?

How this is graded: completion, not a "correct" position — there isn't one, especially for Part 3. Full credit requires all three parts of your own post, plus both replies.
