Module 1
Module 01 Chapter 1

Market Orientation & Customer Lifetime Value

Central Question

"If eliminating your most dissatisfied customers improves your average satisfaction score, does it also improve your firm's profitability?"

The module builds toward what it calls the distributions-versus-averages problem: removing a segment from a distribution changes the average, but not always in the direction you'd bet on. You're led to a specific prediction, then shown data that contradicts it — deliberately.

Module 2
Module 02 Chapter 2

Marketing Metrics and Marketing Profitability

Central Question

"Can a company triple its profits and simultaneously destroy $41 million it never knew it was losing?"

This is the module that answers "how do you actually know?" for everything M01 raised. Conventional metrics — net profit, revenue growth, earnings per share — can conceal a firm's most important marketing failures. The module works through what it calls the product-line drop paradox: a line with negative net profit whose removal would make the company worse off, not better.
