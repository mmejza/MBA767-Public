MBA 767 · Orientation · Item 5

Framework Map Self-Check

6 items · practice only, no grade

What This Checks

Recognition, not memorization. Each item draws on an example from the three videos you just watched — matching, fill-in-the-blank, and short application, all multiple choice or select-all-that-apply.

This is not a test of whether you can recite a definition. It's a check on whether the vocabulary from the videos (Need/Want/Demand/Market, the 5Cs, STP, the 4Ps) is starting to feel usable.

What to Expect

  • Unlimited attempts — this exists to help you check your own understanding, not to be gotten right the first time.
  • Feedback is shown immediately after each attempt, explaining the reasoning, not just marking right or wrong.
  • Does not affect your grade in any way.
If something here doesn't click, that's useful information — not a problem. Every framework you see this week gets a full module of its own later.
