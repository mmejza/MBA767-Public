Practice: Applying the Framework

4 items · practice only, no grade · adapted from an existing exercise sheet

What This Checks

The self-check tested recognition. This tests actual use — select-all-that-apply items where you have to reason, not just recall.

Items 1–3 run the Need → Want chain backward: given a real product, which needs could it plausibly be satisfying? Some options are traps — they sound plausible but don't hold up.

Item 4 covers a full market-segment data table (the same one you'll use formal tools for in Module 3) — which strategic claims about the data are actually well-supported, and which just sound reasonable?

What to Expect

  • Unlimited attempts, feedback shown immediately — same as the self-check.
  • Each item has more than one correct option to select, and at least one deliberate trap.
  • Does not affect your grade in any way.
Two of the product links in Items 1–3 point to real external retail pages. If a link looks broken, it's a dead product listing, not a mistake on your end — the question still works without clicking through.
