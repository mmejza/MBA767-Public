Frequently Asked Questions

MBA 767: Marketing Strategy

Course Structure

How fast does this course move?

The course runs for sixteen weeks, from August 24 through December 9. You should expect to complete one module per week for the first eleven weeks, then a four-week Marketing Plan Project to close the term.

Can I work ahead?

Not by much. Each module opens on its scheduled week, so you can't get more than a few days ahead of the class even if you want to. This is deliberate: at 60 students, letting everyone self-pace through the whole semester would make grading unmanageable, and this isn't an easy textbook or course to work through without the weekly structure keeping you on pace.

Within a module, you should still complete items in the order presented — Gate 1 must be passed before the slide deck viewer opens, and the slide deck viewer must be opened before Gate 2 becomes available. Everything after Gate 2 (lecture, Apps, discussion, case quiz) is a recommended order rather than a technical lock, but later items still assume you've done the earlier ones.

Why don't I see all eleven modules in Canvas yet?

Intro and Modules 1–4 are live from day one. Modules 5–11 are still being finalized and publish on a rolling basis over the first several weeks of the term — each one appears well before its scheduled week, not on the first day of class. This doesn't change any due dates; it just means the later modules aren't visible in Canvas yet.

Where do I find official deadlines?

Canvas is the official source for availability dates, due dates, and closing dates. The Course Roadmap & Key Dates page is a planning aid, but Canvas dates control.

Textbook and Reading

Is the textbook required?

Yes. The textbook is required. Gate 1 Reading Checks, module assignments, discussions, cases, and applied analysis work assume that you have completed the assigned chapter reading.

Can I complete the course by using only slides or videos?

No. Slides and videos reinforce important ideas, but they do not replace the textbook. Gate 1's questions are drawn exclusively from content that does not appear in the slides or lecture video.

Gate Checks and Module Progression

Why is Gate 1 worth zero points?

Gate 1 Reading Checks are prerequisite mastery checkpoints. They do not contribute grading points directly, but they are required because they verify preparation and unlock the slide deck viewer.

What happens if I do not earn the required Gate 1 score?

You need 80% or higher (4 of 5 questions) to pass. You get three attempts, 15 minutes each, with per-answer feedback after every attempt. The slide deck viewer stays locked until you pass.

Can I skip Gate 1 and go straight to the case or discussion?

No. Gate 1 is required to unlock the slide deck viewer, which is required to unlock Gate 2. The case quiz and discussion aren't technically locked behind Gate 2, but they assume you've completed everything before them, including the Apps.

Assignments and Discussions

Are discussions required?

Yes, when assigned. Discussions are part of the module learning process and should be completed professionally and thoughtfully.

Do I have to post before seeing other students' discussion replies?

Some discussions may require students to post before viewing classmates' replies. Follow the settings and instructions for each discussion.

Are case assignments individual work?

Yes, unless the assignment instructions explicitly state otherwise. Students are expected to submit their own analysis and be able to explain their reasoning.

Why are cases challenging?

Cases require you to combine calculations, interpretation, qualitative judgment, and strategic reasoning. They are designed to help you apply marketing concepts rather than simply recognize definitions.

Are the quizzes open-book?

Gate 2 and the Case Analysis Quiz are open-book, open-note: your own textbook and notes are fine to have open. Gate 2 is timed at 20 minutes (30 minutes in Module 3, which has more items); the Case Analysis Quiz is timed at 45 or 50 minutes depending on the module. Gate 1 doesn't have a stated open- or closed-book policy — but its questions are drawn from content that doesn't appear in the slides, so having the book open only helps if you've actually read it.

Open-book isn't the same as open-collaboration: working from your own materials is fine, working with or from another student's answers is not, on any of these.

Grades and Deadlines

How is my final grade determined?

Your final grade is based on the percentage of total available course points you earn. Each module includes graded and non-graded requirements; graded activities contribute approximately 185–195 points per module (Gate 1 is worth 0). The Marketing Plan Project adds points on top of the eleven modules — see the syllabus for the current total.

Will scores be rounded up?

Scores will not be rounded up. Final grades are assigned according to the grading policy in the syllabus.

Will there be a curve?

Students should not assume that a grading adjustment will occur. The instructor reserves the right to apply a grading adjustment at the conclusion of the course if warranted by overall class performance. Any adjustment would be applied consistently and uniformly.

What if I miss a deadline?

The due date is the closing date — there is no separate grace period afterward. Once an item closes, it may not be reopened except under circumstances approved by the instructor. Review the specific dates in the Course Roadmap & Key Dates page and in Canvas, and plan to finish before the deadline, not at it.

The instructor normally responds within 24 hours on weekdays and within 48 hours on weekends or university holidays. During periods of unusually high activity, responses may occasionally require additional time.

What should I check before emailing a question?

Emails sent shortly before a deadline may not receive a response before the deadline passes. Waiting until the final hours before a deadline does not create an obligation for immediate assistance or a deadline extension.

The Marketing Plan Project

What is the Marketing Plan Project?

It's the capstone that closes the semester (Weeks 13–16): you select a company and topic, complete a situation analysis, produce a full draft with peer review, then submit a final marketing plan. It's built to draw on the frameworks from every module that comes before it.

When is the final Marketing Plan due?

The final submission is due before the last week of the term, which is reserved for wrap-up and grading rather than new deliverables. Exact dates are on the Course Roadmap & Key Dates page.

Academic Integrity and AI

Can I use artificial intelligence tools?

No, by default. This course is designed to be AI-resistant, and the assumption for every assignment is that AI tools are not used, unless an assignment's own instructions explicitly say otherwise.

Most of this course is quizzes — calculations and multiple-choice questions (Gate 1, Gate 2, the Apps, the Case Analysis Quiz) — not written prose. "Not using AI" still applies: having a tool compute the number, interpret the scenario, or pick the answer for you counts as AI use, even though what you click or type in is just a number or a letter. Discussions are the only assignments with a written submission, and there, having AI draft or paraphrase any part of your post counts the same way.

Before coursework begins, you'll complete the Start Here Quiz in the Course Information module — it also confirms you've reviewed the syllabus and completed a technology check. One of its three questions is the AI certification, attesting that your work will be completed without AI assistance except where an assignment explicitly permits it. It's the first required item in the course.

Can I work with classmates?

Only when assignment instructions explicitly allow collaboration. Otherwise, quizzes, cases, applied analyses, and written submissions are individual work.
