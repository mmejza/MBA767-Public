Getting Started in MBA 767

Marketing Strategy | Fall 2026

Welcome

Welcome to MBA 767: Marketing Strategy. This course focuses on the quantitative and qualitative analysis of marketing situations. Many students find this course different from prior marketing courses because it emphasizes analytical thinking, strategic reasoning, and evidence-based decision making rather than memorization.

What Makes MBA 767 Different?

Many marketing courses focus primarily on concepts and terminology. MBA 767 focuses on analyzing marketing situations, interpreting quantitative information, evaluating strategic alternatives, and defending recommendations using evidence and sound reasoning — then applying that same reasoning across a full semester to a Marketing Plan Project you build yourself.

Before you begin: This is a full 16-week semester, Aug 24 – Dec 9. You will complete one module per week for eleven weeks, then a four-week Marketing Plan Project to close the term. Success requires steady weekly progress.

Step 1: Read the Course Syllabus

Begin by reading the course syllabus carefully. The syllabus explains course expectations, grading policies, communication expectations, academic integrity requirements, and the overall structure of the course.

Pay especially close attention to the course schedule, grading policy, late work policy, and the explanation of how modules are completed.

Step 2: Understand How the Course Works

MBA 767 consists of 11 content modules completed one per week, followed by a four-week Marketing Plan Project. Each module is designed as a sequence, so students should complete module items in the order shown in Canvas.

Typical Module Sequence

  1. Read the assigned textbook chapter.
  2. Review the module overview page and study guide.
  3. Complete the Gate 1 Reading Check to unlock the slide deck viewer.
  4. Work through the slide deck viewer to unlock Gate 2.
  5. Complete Gate 2, the applied reading check.
  6. Watch the lecture video and complete the three App Worksheet Quizzes.
  7. Complete the module discussion, then the Case Analysis Quiz.
  8. Submit all required work by the posted deadlines.
Gate 1 Reading Checks: Gate 1 Reading Checks do not contribute grading points directly. Instead, they are required mastery checkpoints — you must score 80% or higher to unlock the slide deck viewer, which in turn must be opened before Gate 2 becomes available. Nothing after Gate 2 is technically locked, but the lecture video, Apps, discussion, and case quiz are still meant to be completed in that order.

Step 3: Obtain the Required Textbook

The textbook is required and should be obtained before beginning Module 1. Gate 1 Reading Checks and many module assignments are based on assigned textbook chapters.

Students who delay obtaining the textbook often fall behind during the first week because module work assumes that assigned reading has already been completed.

Required textbook:
Market-Based Management, 6th Edition, by Roger Best.

Step 6: Understand Academic Integrity and Professional Expectations

Academic dishonesty, cheating, plagiarism, and other violations of academic integrity will not be tolerated. Students are expected to complete their own work unless an assignment specifically permits collaboration.

Cases, discussions, quizzes, and applied analysis assignments require authentic student reasoning. This course is designed to be AI-resistant, and the default assumption is that AI tools are not used on any assignment unless its instructions explicitly say otherwise — for the quizzes that make up most of this course (Gates, Apps, the Case Analysis Quiz), that means doing the calculation or reasoning yourself even though what you submit is just a number or a selected option; for Discussions, it means not having AI draft or paraphrase any part of your post. You'll certify this once, at the very start of the course, as part of the Start Here Quiz — see the checklist above.

Students are also expected to communicate professionally in discussions, emails, and all other course interactions.

Final Reminder

Begin early, read carefully, and follow the module sequence. The course is manageable if you keep pace, but it becomes difficult quickly if you fall behind — especially heading into the Marketing Plan Project at the end of the term.
