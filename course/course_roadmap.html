How a Typical Module Week Runs

Every content module (M01–M11) opens Monday. Many students in this course work full time and do most of a module's work Thursday through Sunday — that's a normal pattern here, not a problem, as long as you keep two things in the right order.

The one sequence that's actually enforced by Canvas

Gate 1 must be passed before the slide deck viewer opens. The slide deck viewer must be opened before Gate 2 becomes available. That's it — those are the only two technical locks in the whole module. Do the chapter reading and Gate 1 first, whatever day of the week that ends up being for you.

Strongly recommended, not enforced: Apps before Discussion and the Case Quiz

Nothing in Canvas stops you from opening the Case Analysis Quiz before finishing the three Apps — but each App builds a calculation method the case then asks you to apply on your own, with the case's own numbers, and the case quiz allows only one attempt. Do the Apps first. Same logic for the discussion: it goes more smoothly once you've worked through the module's calculations, not before.

Every item's specific due date is on its own assignment page in Canvas — there's no separate grace period past the due date, so don't assume a missed deadline is recoverable. If you're planning your week around a Thursday–Sunday push, make sure that push starts with the chapter reading and Gate 1, not the case quiz.
